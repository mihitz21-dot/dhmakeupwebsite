## Brand & Style

The design system embodies bespoke bridal artistry, elevated elegance, and timeless romantic warmth. Crafted specifically for luxury bridal and special-event makeup clientele, the aesthetic evokes calmness, bespoke care, and radiance. 

### Design Movements & Visual Style
- **Warm Editorial Minimalism:** Generous, breathable negative space layered with warm ivory and blushing canvas tones rather than cold stark whites.
- **Transitional Sophistication:** Combining authoritative high-fashion editorial serif typography with crisp, readable modern grotesque letterforms.
- **Organic Botanics & Line Art:** Minimalist, hand-drawn floral vectors, delicate star dustings, and butterfly motifs functioning as subtle, non-intrusive backdrop embellishments.
- **Soft Diffusion:** Warm photographic lighting, gentle ambient glow, delicate borders, and softly rounded corners that evoke the texture of fine stationery and bridal keepsakes.

## Colors

The color architecture creates a tactile, luxurious palette inspired by bridal silk, delicate cosmetic powders, and vintage rose accents:

- **Primary (`#B76E79` - Dusty Rose):** Anchors main interactive moments, primary action buttons, focused state tabs, and signature badges.
- **Secondary (`#A85858` - Terracotta Rust):** Provides deeper contrast for active button states, footer CTA banners, and highlight cards.
- **Tertiary (`#D4A373` - Champagne Gold):** Applied sparingly to micro-accents, star icons, review ratings, and decorative flourish dividers.
- **Neutral (`#2C2523` - Muted Espresso Charcoal):** Ensures high-contrast legibility without the harshness of pure black; used for headings, primary text, and luxury dark footer panels.
- **Surfaces & Canvas:** 
  - Primary Background: `#FAF7F5` (Warm Alabaster).
  - Surface Card Background: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp Linen White).
  - Tonal Alt Surfaces: `#F5ECE8` (Blush Shell) for package callouts, feature bars, and tinted section panels.
  - Border Accents: `#E8DDD9` (Subtle Rosy Ochre).

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial rhythm balance:

- **Display & Headings:** `Playfair Display` offers high-contrast classic serifs, graceful terminals, and a timeless bridal aesthetic. It is paired with italic variations for emotive script-like subtitles and pull-quotes.
- **Body & Navigation:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides soft geometric clarity, open counters, and optimal readability across dense service lists, package pricing details, and contact forms.
- **Eyebrow & Sub-headers:** Rendered using `label-caps` in uppercase with letter spacing of `0.15em` to structure sections cleanly without visual clutter.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a responsive 12-column fluid grid, emphasizing generous breathing room to simulate a high-end luxury print lookbook.

### Grid Rhythm & Adapters
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, max content width of `1240px`, with `margin: 4rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Service and feature tiers display in balanced 3 or 4-column groupings.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout, gutters step down to `1.25rem`, with margins at `2rem`. Feature cards wrap gracefully into 2-column matrices.
- **Mobile (&lt; 768px):** 4-column layout, gutters and outer margins set to `1rem` and `1.25rem` respectively. Long horizontal packages shift into soft carousel cards with directional peek indicators.

Vertical cadence relies heavily on ample section spacing (5rem to 7rem between major editorial zones) to preserve tranquility.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, diffuse ambient layers rather than hard dropshadows, ensuring an airy, weightless quality across all viewports.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Applied to the base canvas (`#FAF7F5`) and alternating blush section panels (`#F5ECE8`).
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules):** Pure white container surfaces resting over ivory backgrounds with soft ghost borders (`1px solid #E8DDD9`) and low-opacity ambient shadows: `box-shadow: 0 8px 30px rgba(183, 110, 121, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Focused Cards):** On hover, package cards and service previews lift subtly with: `box-shadow: 0 14px 40px rgba(168, 88, 88, 0.12)` and a smooth `-2px` Y-axis translate.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Navigation & Modals):** Navigation bars use a frosted glass layer: `background: rgba(250, 247, 245, 0.92)` paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a whisper-thin bottom border (`#E8DDD9`).

## Shapes

The design uses gentle organic geometry to reinforce a feminine, welcoming beauty aesthetic.

- **Primary UI & Cards:** `roundedness: 2` (0.5rem / 8px default radii; larger content cards and image frames use `rounded-lg` at 1rem / 16px).
- **Arched Feature Frames:** Hero and portrait photography frequently adopt an architectural, roman-arch top boundary (`border-radius: 200px 200px 16px 16px`) echoing bridal vanity mirrors and classic salon archways.
- **Pill Elements:** Interactive chips, filter pills, and signature CTA buttons use complete pill styling (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to soften cursor and touch interaction.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (CTA):** Pill-shaped, dusty rose fill (`#B76E79`), pure white label in `button-text`, with subtle horizontal padding (`px-6 py-3`). Hover transitions smoothly to deeper terracotta rust (`#A85858`).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent surface with a delicate 1px border in `#B76E79` or `#2C2523`, transitioning to an ultra-light rose tint on hover.
- **WhatsApp / Direct Booking Pill:** Distinctive terracotta surface incorporating micro-iconography with centered white typography.

### Chips & Filter Tabs
- Used across the portfolio gallery (e.g., "All", "Bridal", "Soft Glam", "Traditional", "Editorial").
- Inactive state: Transparent background, `#2C2523` typography, and soft hover feedback.
- Active state: Pill-shaped dusty rose (`#B76E79`) fill with crisp white text.

### Cards & Service Tiers
- **Service Preview Cards:** White card surface (`rounded-lg`), holding a top image with an arched or soft-corner crop, a centered terracotta circular badge with fine line icons, followed by serif service title, concise feature list, and subtle text links with arrow icons.
- **Package Pricing Cards:** Elevated white cards with thin `#E8DDD9` borders. Most popular tier features a subtle top highlight banner (`#B76E79`) and a blush container background (`#FDF8F6`).

### Form Elements & Inputs
- **Text Fields & Dropdowns:** Pill-corner or soft-corner rectangles (`border-radius: 8px`), minimal 1px border in `#E8DDD9`, warm cream-tinted white interior (`#FCFAF9`), and muted charcoal placeholder text. Focus state transitions to a 1px `#B76E79` ring with zero aggressive outer glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Circular and soft-square indicators with subtle terracotta borders, filled with `#B76E79` and white checkmarks when selected.

### Testimonials & Reviews
- Clean card modules with quotation marks stylized in soft muted rose (`#B76E79`), italicized feedback quotes, circular bridal avatar thumbnails, and warm gold five-star rating indicators (`#D4A373`).
